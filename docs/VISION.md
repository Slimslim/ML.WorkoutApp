# Product vision (draft, 2026-11-01)

## In one line
One app that **measures** every workout you do — HYROX, CrossFit (benchmarks, Hero WODs, class
WODs), strength and Olympic lifting — and turns that data into insights and recommendations that
help you improve.

## Who it is for
Hybrid athletes who train in a box or gym, lift, and race (HYROX and similar), and wear an Apple
Watch. First user: the athlete; next: friends via TestFlight; later: the App Store.

## What makes it different
Most apps in this space are either **logs** (you type the score after the WOD: SugarWOD, Beyond
the Whiteboard, WOD Log) or **race-only trackers** (HYROX timers). This app is neither:

1. **The watch measures, you don't type.** Splits from a Crown turn, heart rate and recovery,
   run distance and pace, and from motion: bar path and velocity for lifts, rep counts and
   work/rest for stations (in progress).
2. **All training in one place.** Races, class WODs, benchmarks and lifting sessions share one
   history, so progress and fatigue can be compared across everything you do.
3. **From data to advice.** Not just charts: what changed, why, and what to do next.

## Pillars
| Pillar | What it means | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Track | Watch workout modes: races, WODs (for time, AMRAP, EMOM, rests), lifting with bar path | Built, in testing |
| Measure | Splits, HR + recovery, pace, steps; motion → reps, work/rest, bar speed | Partly built; rep counting in research |
| Review | Phone overview per workout: splits, HR chart, round grid, corrections | Built |
| Compare | Same workout over time, benchmarks/PRs, personal calibration (run distance) | Next |
| Improve | Insights, recommendations, target splits (ahead/behind), readiness (resting HR/HRV) | Planned |

## Near-term focus (to Dec 3–6 2026, HYROX Anaheim)
Race-ready watch mode with target splits, run calibration, TestFlight for 2–3 testers.
After the race: benchmarks/PR history, lifting + WOD unified sessions, insight engine.

## Naming implication
The name should cover all training, not only HYROX or only lifting. "BarPath" can stay as the
name of the lifting feature.
