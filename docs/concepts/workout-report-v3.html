Workout Report v3

How the phone report looks now that the watch gives us every rep, the loads, the time before the first rep and a bar-speed estimate. One layout for every format: score, what stood out, the workout itself, heart rate, then the details. Numbers come from your sessions on Nov 1 and Nov 2 unless a phone is marked as an example.

Same order in every format
  1. Score. Name, the one number that format is scored by, one context line, three tiles and a tiny picture of the workout. Readable from 2 m.
  2. What stood out. Two or three plain sentences, each with the numbers behind it. Only things the data really shows.
  3. The workout. One view shaped like the format: minute slots, rounds, the 40:00 clock, race legs. Tap a row to open its reps.
  4. Heart rate and recovery. The whole session with the work coloured behind it, then the drop at 1, 2 and 3 minutes.
  5. Details. Every rep or split, folded until you ask for it.
  6. Data note. One grey line saying what was found by motion, what you entered and what is an estimate.

Design decisions
  • One skeleton, four stories. Score → what stood out → the workout → heart rate → details → data note, in every format.
  • The first screen answers "how did it go". Score, a tiny picture of the workout and three insights all sit above the dashed line.
  • Hatched = before the first rep, in the colour of what comes next. Start delay in an EMOM, a walk to the bar, Roxzone: one visual rule.
  • Every number says where it came from: ● motion, ✎ entered, ~ estimate. Bar speed is always labelled as a wrist estimate.
  • Insights are plain sentences with their evidence right under them, picked from comparisons with your own numbers only.
Questions for you
  1. EMOM rows: is rest left the right big number, or would you rather see time after the beep (how long you waited before pulling)?
  2. Loads: type them on the phone after the workout (one field per set, copied down), or pick them on the watch before each set?
  3. Bar speed: keep m/s numbers, or show it only relative to yourself ("−17 % from your 125 lb sets")?
  4. AMRAP with alternating options: is comparing A rounds with A rounds (and B with B) how you think about it?
  5. HYROX: should the plan come from a target finish time you type in, or from your last race's splits?
