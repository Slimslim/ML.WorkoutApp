# Copy deck (from the copywriter) — use, edit lightly

NAME: "Wave Mode" (in app: "BarPath Wave"; tagline "Wave Mode by BarPath"). Every racer already says "what wave are you?"; describes the arming feature.

HERO: "Eight runs. Eight stations. Zero taps."  Subline: "Wave Mode arms your watch before your start time and switches itself between running and stations."
Alt: "Race it. Your watch runs the clock." / "Pick your race, strap in, go. BarPath tracks every run, station and Roxzone split."

SECTIONS
- Pick your race — Choose your event on the phone: "Anaheim · Sat Dec 5 · Wave 10:40 · Men's Open." Your watch loads the format, your start time and every station in order.
- Race-day watch — Live pace, run time, km progress and a lap counter ("Lap 2 of 2"). Eyes down for half a second, back to work.
- The Roxzone — The screen goes pulsing pink the moment you're in transition, and that time is clocked on its own. Walk-time is race-time; now you can see it.
- Stations — Each of the eight stations gets its own screen and a haptic on start. SkiErg through Wall Balls, one glance, one cue.
- Finish + overview — Cross the line and get a proper "Well done," your total, and the breakdown: runs, stations, Roxzone, and how much you faded. Heart-rate timeline and per-station times vs your last race are waiting on your phone.
- History and badges — Every race and home simulation (Half HYROX, partner WODs) saved in one place, with PBs per station and badges to chase.
- Join the test — BarPath is a prototype built by one athlete, and your feedback shapes it. Jump on TestFlight before Anaheim and put it through a real session.
HONESTY: "Auto-switching works off wrist motion and has been tested on one athlete so far. Turn the Crown if it ever misses."
