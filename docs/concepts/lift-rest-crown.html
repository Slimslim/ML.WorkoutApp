5. Why a hand shake can't become a set

During Rest the rep counter and the lift detector get no say: their events are ignored, and when the Crown arms they start fresh, counting only motion after the Crown turn. A shake before the Crown turn is never seen. Left over: a shake after arming, before the set, is the same risk as today; and a 0.8-turn Crown turn by accident (sleeve, wrist pressed on the knee) would arm. The owner's device test covers both.
