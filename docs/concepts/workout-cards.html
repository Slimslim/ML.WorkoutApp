Workout Cards

The workout card as the app will use it, built from the header study decisions and writing conventions. First the spec, then the card in six real places in the app. Your own workouts and scores are real; anything else is marked as an example. Colours are placeholders.

1 · Card spec

Anatomy

  1. Date · program on the left (Today, Mon Nov 2, Mar 2023; PitFIRE, Mayhem, Built-in).
  2. Block tag + type tag on the right. Block: Metcon, Strength, Accessory (neutral). Type: Chipper, EMOM, AMRAP, AMREP, Death by, Ladder, RFT, Complex, Sets × reps, For quality (outlined, the one accent).
  3. Name, 2 lines max then "…". Open workouts get the prefix (Open 13.4). No name: the format is the name (Every 3:00 × 3).
  4. Time domain line, first and on its own: <N> min <TYPE> (7 min AMREP, 40 min AMRAP, 5 × 3 min AMRAP · rest 1 min), Every 1:00 × 8 for EMOMs, 5 rounds for RFT. No cap here.
  5. Rep scheme on top, once, when movements share it: 21-15-9, 40-30-20-30-40 Cal.
  6. One movement per line, no dividers: quantity, unit, movement, @load. Movements under a shared scheme carry no reps; fixed reps stay (7 Power Cleans @155 lb).
  7. Cap at the bottom: 20 min cap.
  8. After logging, the score line under one thin rule: score, unit, Rx / Scaled badge, then one comparison (last time or the cap).

Rules

Nothing elseNo coach notes, strategy, stimulus, targets or RPE. The card logs what was done.
Your loadFrom your profile division (no manual pick): 225/155 lb → @225 lb. "See details" shows the original Rx M/F card.
Rx / ScaledAsked after logging, with loads and reps editable. ? (dashed) = not answered yet, as on your older logs.
AbbreviationsHSPU, T2B, C&J, DB, KB, DU; ALT = alternating, Double DB = two dumbbells.
Unitslb never lbs · heights in in · runs in m (miles when the workout says so) · carries in ft · calories Cal before the movement.
Long listsMore than 5 movements: first 4 + +18 more. Logged lifts: one line per set or minute, no limit.
TruncationName 2 lines (compact row 1 line), movement lines never wrap mid-load: the @load stays together.
