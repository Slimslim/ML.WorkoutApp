07 · Doubles, after the race

Team view

Each watch records its own race: your work and rest from wrist motion, your heart rate, your reps, the transitions. The two watches never need to talk. After the race both results sync from the phones to your accounts online, and the app merges them by time into one Team view: who did how much at every station, both heart rates on one chart, and the Roxzone you ran together.
