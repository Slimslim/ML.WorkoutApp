Workout Header Study

One header, "D", built from your round-1 decisions and applied to the same 12 workouts. It holds only what you log: date, source, block, workout type, name, format, one line per movement with your load, and the score once you are done. No notes, strategy or stimulus. Round 1 (A, B, C) is in the repo history.

Header D, top to bottom
  1. Date · source on the left; block tag (Metcon, Strength, Accessory) and type tag on the right.
  2. Name, 2 lines max. No name: built from the movements, in lighter text.
  3. Format line: scheme · cap · rest. Structure only.
  4. Rep scheme once on top when it is shared (21-15-9), then movements, one line each, no dividers: 12 DB C&J @40 lb.
  5. Score after the workout, under one thin rule: score · Rx/Scaled · one comparison.

Rules
No notesNothing from the coach's text: no strategy, stimulus, scaling advice, targets, RPE or stories. The header is a log of what was done.
DateToday, Yesterday, Mon Nov 2, older years Mar 2023.
SourceOne short label: Mayhem, Class, Benchmark, Open, Your library.
Two tagsBlock first (neutral fill), type second (outlined, the one accent). Always both, always in that order.
Name2 lines max, then "…". Quoted names keep their quotes. Lift titles split: Front Squat : 3-3-3-3-3 → name Front Squat, format 5 × 3. No name → movements joined with "+", lighter text.
Rep scheme on topA scheme shared by all movements is written once, above them: 21-15-9, 3-3-3-3-3, 40-30-20-30-40 Cal. Movements below it carry no reps; a movement with its own fixed reps keeps them (7 Power Cleans @155 lb).
WritingOne movement per line, written the way you write it: quantity, unit, movement, @load. 12 Cal Row, 15 Push-ups, 12 DB C&J @40 lb, 1:00 Suitcase Hold @pick, 50 ft Front-Rack Carry. Loads are always inline after "@", never in a column.
Your loadM/F resolved to your division (135/95 lb → @135 lb), %1RM to real weight, kg to lb. More than 5 movements: first 4 + +18 more. The full list of conventions lives in HEADER_STUDY.md and grows as we see more workouts.
ScoreTime, rounds + reps, reps, heaviest set (volume second), or "Done". Then Rx/Scaled, then one comparison: last time or the cap.
