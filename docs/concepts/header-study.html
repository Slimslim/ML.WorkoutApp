Workout Header Study

The first block at the top of every workout, before and after you do it: date, name, movements, summary. Three ways to lay it out, each applied to the same 12 workouts (9 from programming apps, 3 of yours with real scores), so you can compare them row by row. Lighter type, neutral surfaces, one accent. Colours are placeholders.

Variant AEditorial: the name leadsDate and source on top, the name as the title, one format line, movements as a sentence with your loads. Score sits under a thin rule once you are done.
Variant BFormat-led: what you will do is the heroThe name shrinks to a label; "21-15-9 for time" becomes the big line. Movements as a small list with reps and load right-aligned. After the workout the score replaces the format as the hero.
Variant CCard and chips: compact, scannableName with the score on the right, format and movements as chips, and a thin timeline that shows the shape (cap, rounds and rests, minutes, sets). Built for lists and the watch handoff.

Rules every variant follows
DateToday Yesterday, then Mon Nov 2 this year, Mar 2023 for older results. Done workouts add the finish time only on the Live tab.
SourceOne short label: the program (Mayhem), Class, Benchmark, Open or Your library. Never the coach's notes, strategy or dedication story; those stay behind "More".
Name, 2 lines maxEnds with "…" after two lines; the full name is one tap away. Quoted names keep their quotes. Lift titles split: Front Squat : 3-3-3-3-3 → name Front Squat, format 5 × 3.
No nameGenerated from the movements (Double Unders + AbMat Sit-ups), shown in the lighter text colour so you can tell it apart from a real name.
Benchmark tagOnly for real benchmarks (Diane, The Chief) and Open workouts (Open 13.4), because those have a history to compare with.
Your loadM/F pairs resolve to your division, picked once: 135/95 lb → 135 lb. Heights and KB too (24/20 in → 24 in). %1RM becomes real weight from your stored max: 65–75 % → 165–190 lb.
UnitsYour unit everywhere (lb here). A source in kg is converted, original in brackets on tap: 85 kg → 187 lb.
Format lineSmall grammar, always in this order: kind · scheme · cap/rest · target. For time · 21-15-9 · cap 8:00, 5 × AMRAP 3:00 · rest 1:00, Every 1:00 × 15.
Score afterThe type follows the format: time, rounds + reps, reps, heaviest set (volume second), minutes done, or just "Done". Then Rx/Scaled, then one comparison: last time, target or cap.
Recommendation

Pick A (Editorial) as the standard header, and borrow two things from C: the movement chips when a workout has 3 or more movements, and the thin timeline for interval formats (EMOM, cycles, AMRAP blocks) only.

  • A survives every hard case in the inventory: long quoted names, no name, lift days and benchmarks all read the same way, top to bottom.
  • The name is what you remember and search for ("Freedom", "Diane"); B hides it, which hurts in lists and history.
  • B shines for unnamed or lift workouts but breaks for chippers: "22 × 22 for time" says less than the name.
  • C is the best list row and watch preview, but as the header of a screen it gets busy with 4+ chips plus a timeline.
Questions for you
  1. Your division for loads: set it once in Settings (Men's), or switch per workout?
  2. Lift days: lead with the heaviest set (205 lb) and show total volume small underneath. Is that how you think about them?
  3. Long names: two lines then "…" with the full name on tap, or always show the full name on the workout screen?
  4. Source label: show it on every workout, or only when it is not one you made yourself?
