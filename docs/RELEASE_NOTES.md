# TestFlight release notes

Newest first. Paste the latest entry into "What to Test" for the build in App Store Connect.

## Build 15

- If the watch app closes or the watch restarts mid-workout, what was recorded is saved and sent to the phone.
- Long recordings upload (compressed, nothing lost).
- Turning the Crown just as a rest ends no longer skips a part.
- Lifting no longer gets stuck on "Ending…".
- Deleting your account removes every uploaded file.
- Weights in kg or lb, and your height and body details in Settings.

## Build 14

- Watch: with your wrist down the clocks show the seconds, not 1:--.

## Build 13

- Results corrected in the cloud now open on the phone.
- A free workout keeps the name it was given.

## Build 12

- Pull down on Log to bring in results corrected in the cloud.

## Build 11

- Results page writes the workout like the whiteboard, one thing per line.

## Build 10

- EMOM results: heaviest set, load, start time and rest for each minute.
- Monday no longer shows a finished workout twice.
- A 1 mile run now reads 1 mile, not 1609 m.
- Heart rate numbers moved to the left of the chart.
- Smaller dot in the avatar menu.

## Build 9

- Heart rate through the workout is back on the results page: bands, zones, tap to read, and recovery.

## Build 8

- New workout results page: score, time by modality, Effort, rounds, heart rate and recovery.
- Avatar menu: Open on watch with a green or orange dot, Settings, Sign out.
- Settings shows the app version and build at the bottom.

## Build 7

- The + stays round when pressed.
- AMRAP rounds count correctly when the clock ends during a rest.
- Effort uses the highest heart rate ever recorded.

## Build 6

- New navigation: Today, Workouts, +, Log and Progress.
- Effort, Settings behind the avatar, Live opens by itself.
